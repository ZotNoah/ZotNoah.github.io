About Me

Hello, and welcome to my Webiste!

My name is Noah Unverzagt, and I recently graduated with a degree in Web Development from Metropolitan Community College in Omaha, Nebraska. I am actively seeking an entry-level position as a Front-End Developer, where I can apply my skills, contribute to real-world projects, and continue growing professionally.

While completing my degree, I worked at Nebraska Furniture Mart, where I also completed an internship as a developer. In addition to academic coursework, I've gained valuable hands-on experience through two internships that expanded my practical knowledge of the field.

At Childhood Cancer Society, I was involved in the design process and collaborated with a team to bring creative concepts to life. During my internship at Nebraska Furniture Mart, I worked within an Agile development environment and was introduced to backend technologies such as ASP.NET and SQL.

I am passionate about front-end development and excited to join a professional team where I can contribute, learn, and grow as a developer.
